Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

We don't collect your data.
Meals Made Easy does not collect, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
Your meals stay yours.
Your meals, photos, plans and shopping list are stored on your device and, if iCloud is turned on, synced through your own private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. The developer cannot see or access this data.
Recipe import.
When you import a recipe from a link, the app downloads that web page directly from the website to read the recipe. Nothing is sent to the developer.
Recipe sharing.
If you choose to share a recipe, the recipe details you share (meal name, ingredients, steps, an optional photo and the sender name you enter) are stored temporarily in Apple's CloudKit public database so the person you send it to can open it in the app or on the web. Anyone with the share link can view that recipe. Shared recipes expire automatically and are not linked to any account. The developer does not use this data for analytics, advertising or tracking. Nothing is shared unless you tap Share.
Restaurant search.
If you search for a restaurant when adding a meal, the search text is sent to Apple Maps to find matching places. The app does not track your location.
Photos.
The app only accesses photos you choose to add to a meal.
Backups.
Backup files are created only when you choose Export Backup, and they are saved wherever you choose.
Children.
The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
